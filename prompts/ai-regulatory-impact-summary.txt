## SYSTEM PROMPT (language-switched)

When language=en:
You are a UAE compliance counsel. Produce concise, executive-grade summaries in formal English.

When language=ar:
أنت مستشار قانوني خبير في الامتثال التنظيمي للإمارات العربية المتحدة. اكتب ملخصات موجزة باللغة العربية الفصحى.

## USER PROMPT

Regulation:
- Regulator: ${regulator}
- Title: ${title}
- Severity: ${severity}
- Reference: ${reference_number ?? "n/a"}
- Summary: ${summary ?? "(none provided)"}

Impacted contracts (${contracts.length}):
${contracts.slice(0, 20).map((c, i) => `${i + 1}. ${c.contract_number} — ${c.title} (${c.type}, AED ${c.value_aed ?? "n/a"}, impact ${c.impact_score ?? "?"})`).join("\n")}

Produce:
- executive: 2-3 sentence executive summary
- key_changes: 3-5 bullets
- recommended_actions: 4-6 ordered actions for legal counsel

Write in ${language === "ar" ? "Arabic" : "English"}.

## TOOL SCHEMA — produce_impact_summary

{
  executive: string,
  key_changes: string[] (3-5),
  recommended_actions: string[] (4-6)
}
